Our Processing Operational Workflows

Learn exactly how our Scrap Buy-Back system converts materials cleanly into capital.

Step 1: Secure Delivery & Arrival

Lot Offloading

Clients bring their scrap metals to our depot bays, or one of our registered bakkie drivers delivers a scheduled pickup batch directly to our entrance lanes.

Step 2: Material Evaluation

Sorting & Grading Identification

Our sorting operators clean, separate, and check the quality of incoming batches, splitting ferrous compounds from high-value copper, brass, and aluminum pieces.

Step 3: Precise Measurement

Calibrated Mass Weighing

The sorted metal goes onto certified floor scales. Weights are tracked publicly on screens to verify the exact measurement breakdown down to the final gram.

Step 4: Prompt Trade Finalization

Instant Payout Settlement

Pout value totals are generated using our current base rate per kilogram. We then process direct digital bank transfers or immediate cash vouchers securely on site.
